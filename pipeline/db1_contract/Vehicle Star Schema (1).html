Rules

  1. Shared dimensions are owned by the platform. The vehicle service references them and never creates its own camera, date, run or vehicle-type table.
  2. Every fact carries org_id, run_key, camera_key, date_key, ts and uid. A composite foreign key (run_key, org_id, camera_key) → dim_run guarantees a row's org and camera always match its run.
  3. Times are absolute timestamptz, never frame numbers or seconds into a video.
  4. Images are stored in object storage (MinIO / S3). The database keeps only (bucket, object_key) in media_object, never a URL or a local path.
  5. Every recognition records the ML model (model_key) that produced it.
  6. A failed plate read is still a row, with outcome = unreadable or no_plate. That is why its text, category, confidence and crop may be null.
  7. The make can be known without the model, never the reverse. (vehicle_model_key, manufacturer_key) in fact_vehicle_make is a composite foreign key to dim_vehicle_model: a car model can never be stored under the wrong maker.
  8. plate_text is personal data: it has its own retention and access rule.
  9. Vehicle type is the shared dim_class (class_group = vehicle). model_key always means the ML model; the car model is vehicle_model_key.
